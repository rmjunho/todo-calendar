방법 1. 앱에서 직접 삭제 (즉시 처리)

승인된 계정으로 로그인할 수 있다면 앱 안에서 바로 지울 수 있습니다. 요청이나 대기 없이 누르는 즉시 삭제됩니다. 로그인이 되지 않는다면 아래 방법 2를 이용하세요.

  • 위험 구역 → 계정 삭제를 누르고, 확인을 위해 PIN을 한 번 더 입력합니다.

  • 가입 신청 후 승인을 기다리는 중이거나 거절된 경우
  • 앱(또는 즐겨찾기)을 이미 삭제한 경우
  • PIN을 잊어 로그인할 수 없는 경우

Option 1. Delete it yourself in the app (immediate)

If you can sign in with an approved account, you can delete it from inside the app. There is no request or waiting — it happens the moment you confirm. If you cannot sign in, use option 2 below.

  • Sign in to Todo Calendar with your name and PIN.
  • Tap Settings at the top right.
  • Tap Danger zone → Delete account and enter your PIN once more to confirm.

Option 2. Request by email

Even if your sign-up was not approved, or you have already removed the app, you can request deletion at the address below. In the following situations you cannot sign in and so cannot reach option 1, which makes this address the only route.

  • Your request is awaiting approval or was rejected
  • You have already removed the app (or the bookmark)
  • You forgot your PIN and cannot sign in
